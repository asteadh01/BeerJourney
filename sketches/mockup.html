Batch №014
Smoked Porter

Take Three

We've brewed this one twice before and never nailed the smoke balance. This round cuts the smoked malt to 12% of the grist and adds a cold crash before kegging — much cleaner campfire note, less ashtray.

Brew-day process

  1. 01Mashed grain bill at 152°F for 60 min, single infusion.
  2. 02Sparged to collect 7 gal of wort, boiled 60 min.
  3. 03Hop additions at 60, 15 and 5 min — schedule below.
  4. 04Chilled to 66°F, pitched Wyeast 1056, fermented 12 days.
  5. 05Cold-crashed 48 hrs, kegged and force-carbed at 12 psi.

Notes on this sketch

  • Public side needs no account: dashboard, per-beer recipe pages, embedded brew-day videos, and open commenting.
  • /login is intentionally unlinked from the main nav — reachable only by typing the path, as you described.
  • Admin side is where recipes, photos, and video links get written and published — capped at two Firebase Auth accounts, no public sign-up.
  • Comment moderation would live under the admin's Comments tab, since public comments need at least basic spam/abuse review.
  • Palette and type are placeholders for direction — copper/amber for the brand accent, hop green for status, warm parchment ground; happy to push this further once you've picked real photos and a name.
