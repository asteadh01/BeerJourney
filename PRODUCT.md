# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Homebrewers and the broader craft beer community. People who brew at home and want to follow along with another brewery's recipes, process, and honest notes. Readers range from curious beginners to experienced brewers comparing techniques.

## Product Purpose

Bitácora Cervecera is a public brewing journal for two Argentine homebrewers. Every batch is documented: recipe, process, tasting notes, and whatever went wrong. The goal is a live record that other brewers find useful and that the two authors look back on with satisfaction.

## Brand Personality

Warm, precise, honest. The journal of two people who take their craft seriously without taking themselves too seriously. Not showy. Substance over performance.

## Anti-references

- Hipster brewery sites: heavy kraft paper textures, distressed fonts, excessive effort to look effortless
- SaaS dashboards: clinical whites, blue primaries, metric cards, KPI strips everywhere

## Design Principles

1. Substance over decoration: every visual element should help communicate the batch data or the story, not dress it up
2. Warm but organized: the color warmth signals craft and pleasure; the layout signals precision and care
3. Respect the reader: clear hierarchy, readable type, no mystery about where to look next
4. Show the beer: imagery of the actual product earns more trust than any illustration or icon
5. Consistency of voice: same tone across the journal, admin, and empty states

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Comfortable body type sizes. Sufficient contrast for all text. Prefers-reduced-motion respected.
